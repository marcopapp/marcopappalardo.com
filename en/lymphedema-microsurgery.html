Lymphedema Microsurgery

Diagnosis, staging and individualized treatment.

Treatment selection is based on the functional condition of the lymphatic system in each limb, integrating clinical evaluation and dedicated imaging.

01

Diagnosis & Imaging

Clinical Assessment

Evaluation of disease evolution, infections, symptoms and impact on quality of life.

Lymphoscintigraphy

Functional assessment of lymphatic drainage and support for disease staging.

ICG Lymphography

Superficial mapping of lymphatic patterns and identification of vessels for microsurgical planning.

Lymphatic Ultrasound

Targeted study of lymphatic collectors and vascular structures useful for operative planning.

02

Treatment

Not every lymphedema requires the same operation.

01

LVA / LVB

Lymphovenous anastomosis for selected patients with functioning lymphatic collectors.

02

VLNT

Vascularized lymph node transfer for more advanced disease or when usable lymphatic collectors are absent or severely compromised.

03

Complex Decongestive Therapy

An essential component of treatment, used alone or as part of a broader strategy in selected patients.

03

Limb-specific approach

Bilateral lymphedema is not necessarily symmetric.

Each limb may show a different degree of lymphatic dysfunction and is therefore staged and treated independently according to functional imaging and disease severity.
