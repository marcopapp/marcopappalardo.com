About

My work brings together advanced reconstructive techniques, careful clinical judgment, and long-term partnership with patients.

I focus on restoring function and quality of life through individualized treatment. Every plan begins with a clear diagnosis and an honest discussion of the most appropriate options—from conservative care to microsurgical reconstruction.

Research

Clinical questions should lead to better decisions at the bedside.

My research explores lymphatic imaging, disease staging, treatment selection, and outcomes in reconstructive microsurgery. The goal is practical: clearer pathways, earlier intervention, and care tailored to each patient—and, in bilateral disease, to each limb.
